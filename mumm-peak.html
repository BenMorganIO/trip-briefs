Route Overview

Mumm Peak (2,964 m) rises north of Berg Lake on the Alberta-BC boundary. The standard route is a difficult scramble from Berg Lake Campground via the closed Mumm Basin trail, ascending the south rib and returning via the eastern Mumm Basin Trail and Robson Pass.

Key Facts

Summit: 2,964 m. The uploaded route GPX records the summit waypoint at 53.191094N, 119.147922W, elevation 2,964.8 m. This is the coordinate used by the map and live weather.

Stats: 14.3 km round trip, 1,400 m gain, approximately 10 hours, Difficult (Kane) / T6 (SAC), with a few 5.4 moves near the true summit.

Sources: Spectacular Mountains, Jul 27, 2025; BC Geographical Names.

Route Hazards

Mumm Peak is not a hiking trail. Conditions and technical ability must be assessed independently before committing.

!

Rockfall on the closed Mumm Basin trail High

The trail has been obliterated by rockfall; the debris field has unstable blocks, gaps, and cracks.

Source: Spectacular Mountains, Jul 27, 2025
!

Loose gully High

The lower cliff-band weakness is described as horribly loose and rubbly. Helmets are strongly advised, especially with multiple climbers.

Source: Spectacular Mountains, Jul 27, 2025
!

Exposed summit ridge and 5.4 moves Critical

A short 3-4 m north-side traverse is extremely exposed. The true summit includes a few 5.4 moves. Vern Dewit's report describes the west-side chimney and overhanging chockstone.

Sources: Spectacular Mountains; Vern Dewit / Explor8ion
!

Snow slope near summit Moderate

SummitPost notes a roughly 100 m year-round snow slope; an ice axe is recommended and a rope may aid descent.

Source: SummitPost
